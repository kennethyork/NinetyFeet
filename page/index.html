What you get

Play it or run it

Three ways to hit and two ways to pitch if you want the bat in your hands — or sit in the office and never touch a controller. Both are the same league.

A whole organisation

Twenty-six men on the roster and sixty-six more across three levels of farm system. Contracts, arbitration, the draft, free agency, a luxury tax, injuries, morale, a hall of fame.

869 different ballplayers

No two share a name, a face, or a rating sheet — checked, not claimed. Each with a biography written from his own numbers.

A season with a friend

Two owners, one league, over the wire. Each plays his own club; the results cross, and both machines check the entire league every night so a disagreement surfaces the same day.

Make it yours

Rename and recolour any club, supply your own names for all 832 players from a text file, and rebuild any ballpark down to the fence distances — a fence you move really does change the baseball.

A league your size

8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28 or 32 clubs, drawn evenly from all four divisions. Seasons of 33, 81 or 162 games.

Before you buy

No licensed clubs and no real players. All thirty-two clubs are originals in real markets and every player is invented — the baseball is measured against the real thing, the names are ours.
