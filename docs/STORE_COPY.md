# Store Copy and Capture Plan

## Short description

Play every pitch, build a fictional baseball dynasty, and carry every mode forward automatically.

## Long description

Ninety Feet is an independent arcade baseball game built on a detailed, deterministic simulation.
Step into the box with three hitting styles, paint the corners with classic or meter pitching, and
take control in the field. Then run the organization: set lineups, develop prospects, negotiate
contracts, trade, draft and chase championships across multiple seasons.

Choose a quick Exhibition, manage a Season or Dynasty, follow one athlete in Career, collect and
trade fictional player cards, tackle scripted Moments, or connect directly for online play. Every
playstyle keeps its own progress and selections so reopening the game takes you back to what you
were doing. The included clubs, players and artwork are fictional, and optional local roster
imports remain on the player's device.

## Feature bullets

- Direct batting, pitching, fielding and baserunning with touch, controller or keyboard and mouse
- 8–32 club seasons plus multi-year Dynasty management
- Trades, contracts, drafts, prospects, injuries, records, awards and Hall of Fame
- Career, Collection, Moments, Exhibition and direct-connect Online modes
- Per-mode autosave and automatic recovery backups
- Large text, high contrast, reduced motion, vibration and gameplay-assist settings
- Fictional content with optional user-supplied local roster imports

## Screenshot shot list

Capture at native 16:9 without debug overlays: a close batting view with timing feedback; the pitch
meter and strike zone; a diving fielding play; a Dynasty trade screen; standings and club identity;
the Moments list with progress; the accessibility settings; and a phone screenshot showing touch
controls. Do not show third-party names, logos, notifications, IP addresses or placeholder text.

## Trailer outline

Use 45–60 seconds: open on pitch-to-contact action, show batting/pitching/fielding variety, cut to
league management and trades, accelerate through a season into a championship, then show the mode
lineup and the title. Use captured game audio or properly licensed music only. End with supported
platforms and an accurate availability statement.

## Disclosures before publishing

The current Windows package is unsigned and Android is store-ready only after the release signing
secrets are configured. Online play is direct-connect rather than hosted matchmaking. Verify the
privacy notice, age/content questionnaire, controller claims, install size and hardware support on
each store immediately before submission.
